- This pack adds additional skins that players may choose from, in addition to the default skins or previously used skins!
- Most of these skins reference other games, pop-culture, and pieces of Minecraft's history!
- Some skins are redrawn or touched up to fit the artstyle of this modpack, so no matter which you choose you wont look out of place!
- For the sake of convinence, the skins are sorted into different SERIES based on their design or concept!
- All included skins are the DEFAULT player model.
- A few skins make use of the EARS mod (https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ears) to add additional detail!
- A few skins may use the hat layer!
- Feel free to suggest other skins, I am very open to adding more! 
- This may be updated occasionally, be sure to check in whenever the modpack updates!
